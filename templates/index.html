How it works

Cash-and-carry = buy the stock and sell its future at the same time — you earn the price difference (the basis). This tool shows whether it beats your fixed deposit after taxes, charges and the cost of your capital.

  1. Scan — screen every NSE stock future and rank them by net annualized return after charges.
  2. Analyze — open any stock to see the full trade estimate with your own assumptions.
  3. Decide — compare against a fixed deposit or savings account and check the POSITIVE EDGE / SMALL EDGE / NO EDGE signal.
