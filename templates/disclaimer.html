Summary

This application is a read-only calculator and scanner. It fetches public market data (spot and futures prices) through a broker's market-data API and lets you run your own arithmetic: basis, annualized return, estimated taxes and exchange charges, opportunity cost versus a fixed deposit or savings account, and a simple mathematical comparison signal (POSITIVE EDGE / SMALL EDGE / NO EDGE).

Everything it displays is an estimate computed from your own inputs and third-party data. It is not a statement of what will happen in the market, not a prediction, and not a suggestion that you should buy, sell, or hold any security. Cash-and-carry — like every strategy involving securities or derivatives — involves real risk, including loss of capital.
